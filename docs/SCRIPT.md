# NGMI — Script

Rules: parody names only; short, clip-friendly lines; jokes about the trend, never about real people's real lives. See [CHARACTERS.md](CHARACTERS.md) for voices.

Format: *[stage direction]* · **CHARACTER:** line

---

## Opening cutscene

1. *[Black screen. A phone notification buzzes.]*
2. **AD VOICE:** "Are you a 5? Want to be a 10? Free jawline consultation. Tonight only. Ascension Clinic."
3. *[Kevin stands outside a building glowing sick green. Neon sign flickers: ASCENSION CLINIC – OPEN 24/7 – NO REFUNDS.]*
4. **KEVIN:** "Bro, I just wanted a haircut."
5. *[Doors slam behind him. A ring light switches on by itself.]*
6. **SPEAKER** *(cheerful, like a phone assistant)*: "Welcome! Your rating is… five. Mid. Escape before sunrise, or stay mid forever."
7. *[Fog. Kareem Shammy steps out in a robe and sheet mask.]*
8. **KAREEM SHAMMY:** "Is that… a two-step routine? Oh no. Oh no no no. Follow me. And do NOT touch the cleanser."

## Endings

| Ending | How to get it | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ascend** | Escape with rating 9+ | Kevin walks out glowing. Nobody notices. A dog sniffs him and leaves. Card: "ASCENDED. Still has work at 9." |
| **Cope** | Escape with rating under 4 | Kevin whispers "it's the lighting." Every light behind him goes out. Card: "COPE ENDING." |
| **Jester** | Make 20+ NPCs laugh with emotes | The cast crowns him Jestermaxxer. **Clavicoolar:** "His face is mid, but his bits are elite." |
| **Touch Grass** (secret) | On Level 1, walk back out of the front door within 60 s | Credits roll at once over birdsong. Card: "You were always fine. NGMI? Nah. You made it." |

The Touch Grass ending is intentional: the game's quiet message is that the obsession is the real horror.
