# NGMI — Legal and safety rules

Not legal advice. These are the team's working rules to lower risk. Get a lawyer's review before the Steam launch.

## 1. Parody of real people

The cast is inspired by real looksmaxxing influencers. Using real names, faces or voices in a paid product can bring right-of-publicity or defamation claims, and store takedowns.

**Do:**
- Use only parody names (see [CHARACTERS.md](CHARACTERS.md)).
- Design original caricature faces with exaggerated features.
- Joke about the **trend** (mewing, ratings, mogging, skincare, jawlines).
- Put "Parody. Not affiliated with any real person." in credits and on the store page.

**Don't:**
- Use real names in game, store page, trailer, tags, achievements, or ads.
- Use real photos, voice clips, voice clones, face scans, or deepfakes.
- Reference real people's real-life arrests, hospital visits, drug use, or relationships.
- Imply any real person endorses the game.

## 2. Harmful-behaviour content

- Bone smashing and "hardmaxxing" are **cartoon slapstick** only. No realistic injuries, no how-to.
- No real drugs, peptides, steroids or injections as items. Use clearly fictional items ("Jaw Juice").
- No disordered-eating or self-harm jokes.
- Consider a short opening card: "This is a parody. Please don't hit yourself with hammers."

## 3. Asset licenses

- **CC0** (Poly Haven, ambientCG): free for commercial use, no credit required.
- **Fab Standard License** (Megascans, Fab packs): commercial use in any engine; don't redistribute raw assets (so this repo stays private).
- **CC-BY** (some OpenGameArt): credit the author in game credits.
- **Unity Asset Store EULA** packs: skipped for this UE5 project.
- Every asset needs a row in [ASSETS.md](ASSETS.md) before commit.

## 4. Privacy

- Mic mode: process audio on-device only; never store or upload.
- Streamer chat mode: read chat only for votes; don't store usernames beyond the session.
- Analytics (if any): anonymous, opt-out, disclosed in the privacy policy.

## 5. Repo

- Keep this repository **private** (it may contain licensed assets that can't be redistributed). Exception: owner-approved public window 2026-11-17 → 2026-11-24 (D11). During it, **don't commit any Fab/Megascans or paid assets**, and remember the internal "Inspired by" column in CHARACTERS.md is public too.
- Never commit API keys, tokens or passwords.
