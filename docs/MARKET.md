## Costs
- Steam Direct: $100 per game. It is credited back after $1K revenue.
- Steam cut: 30% (25% after $10M, 20% after $50M).
- UE5: free until $1M lifetime gross, then a 5% royalty.
- US withholding for India sellers: ~15% with a W-8BEN (treaty rate). Confirm with a CA.
- Everything else: free tools and AI, and the owner's PC (RTX 4060).
