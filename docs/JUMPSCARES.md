# NGMI — Jump-scare map

Last updated: 2026-11-17

23 placed jump scares across the campaign (JS_00 in the prologue drive, then JS_01–JS_22). Each one is marked **⚡JS_xx** in [SCRIPT.md](SCRIPT.md) at the exact moment it happens.

**The NGMI rule:** every scare is followed by a laugh within ~10 seconds. Scare → breath → joke. The joke releases the tension so the next scare lands again.

---

## Rules

1. **One big scare every 6–10 minutes** of play. Smaller "creeps" (a sound, a shadow, a head turn) fill the gaps.
2. **Never two big scares back to back.** At least one quiet minute between them.
3. **Build up first.** Silence the music 3–5 seconds before; let ambience and footsteps carry it.
4. **The laugh is designed, not optional.** Every row below has its "release".
5. **Fake-outs are allowed** (about 1 in 5), but the next real scare must come soon after so players don't relax completely.
6. **Floor 5 is the scariest floor.** Floor 6 is the breather (only 2 scares, both half-funny).
7. **Accessibility:** a Jump-scare intensity setting (Full / Reduced / Off). *Reduced* lowers the audio sting by 12 dB and removes screen shake; *Off* replaces the sting with a soft chime and skips sudden zooms. The photosensitivity setting removes strobe flashes (JS_16, JS_22 use a fade instead).
8. **Never gore.** Scares come from timing, sound, movement and wrong-looking faces, never from injury.

Types: **Big** (sting + sudden movement) · **Creep** (slow, wrong, no sting) · **Fake-out** (scare that turns out harmless) · **Boss intro**.

## Background "creeps" (use freely between the big ones)

- A ring light flickers off as you pass, then back on.
- Distant footsteps that stop when you stop.
- A Ghoul's hoodie visible at the end of a corridor, gone when you look again.
- A mannequin's head turned one notch further than last time.
- The Concierge says your name very quietly instead of "Number Five".
- A phone on the floor lights up: "1 new message: behind you". It's a Jaw Juice ad.
